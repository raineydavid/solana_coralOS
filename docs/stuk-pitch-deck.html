On-Chain Oracle Market — an agent that sells a counterparty trust score, and the market that prices, verifies, and settles it. No human in the loop.

The customer · why now

The customer is another agent —
and it has a question before every payment.

Agents have wallets now. They pay each other at machine speed. Before an agent sends money it must know: is this counterparty real — funded, active — or an empty throwaway that takes the money and no-shows?

Guess wrong and you lose the whole transaction. The question has to be answered per payment, in milliseconds. No human is in the loop to answer it — so someone should sell the answer.

This market only exists once agents hold wallets and transact autonomously. On devnet, they now do.
